Today Card

One card for the day. Your date, one intention, one task — and a streak that counts the days you showed up.

Open today’s card → or install it

No feed, no inbox, no backlog. One card, and then you close it.

Why it is only one card

  • The constraint is the feature. A list invites negotiation; one task does not.
  • Nothing leaves the device. No account, no server, no telemetry. Your streak lives in your browser and nowhere else.
  • Works with the network off. Once it has loaded it needs nothing.
