Your date, one intention, one task — and a streak that counts the days you showed up.

Why it is only one card

The constraint is the feature.

A list invites negotiation; one task does not.

No feed, no inbox, no backlog. One card, and then you close it.

What it refuses to do

Nothing leaves the device

No account, no server, no telemetry. Your streak lives in your browser and nowhere else.

It works with the network off

Once it has loaded it needs nothing — open it on a train and it still opens.

It does not nag

No notifications, no streaks you can lose by being ill. The count is a record, not a debt.
